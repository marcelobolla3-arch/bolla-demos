Collect technical enquiries, without a diagnosis

This static script shows what an enquiry about manufacturing or spare parts can look like. Two public sources stay separate: KST Berlin, manufacturing page, and Jonas & Redmann, after-sales. Example only: no request is sent. Every reply is written in advance. It names a public source and the date, or it says the team must check. Nothing is sent and nothing is booked.
