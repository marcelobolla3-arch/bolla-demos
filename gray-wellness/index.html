How it would work for Gray Wellness Club

01

Instant answers, any hour

On your WhatsApp Business number and as a chat on graywellness.com, in English and Arabic. Trained on your classes (Reformer beginner & advanced, Mat Pilates, Vinyasa Yoga, Boxing, HYROX, Powerlifting, Step Up, Sound Healing, ladies-only Booty Lab, Burn and Dance Fit), recovery, The Dose and your house rules.

02

Bookings, not just replies

Complimentary visits and first classes are booked directly into your current booking system, the same schedule members use in the SEVEN+ app, with confirmation and a reminder (arrive 10 minutes early, valet via the app).

03

Every lead captured

Name, phone, interest and preferred time are saved to the lead board and to your CRM, so the team sees who enquired, when and what about.

04

Automatic follow-up

If someone asks and doesn't book, a polite follow-up goes out after 24 hours (and once more later, if you want). Timing and wording are agreed with you; "stop" ends it.

05

Hand-off to staff

Membership plans and pricing, corporate requests, health questions (e.g. hyperbaric or PEMF with a condition) and anything unusual go to the right person with the conversation summarised. Staff can take over the chat at any time.

06

Simple reporting

A dashboard of enquiries, visits and classes booked, follow-ups and joins, by channel and language, so you can see what the assistant brings in.

Integration: connects to your current booking system and CRM via their API (or an n8n automation where no API is available), to WhatsApp Business API and to your website. Your existing join flow (join.graywellness.com) and complimentary-visit page (visit.graywellness.com) stay as they are; the assistant links to them or fills them in for the prospect. Set-up: discovery call, configuration, testing with your team, go-live and hand-over.
