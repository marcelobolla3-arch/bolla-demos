Example Dental Clinic · Dubai

Fictional premium clinic · website & WhatsApp assistant for after-hours and other-time-zone enquiries – free demo

Try a conversation

Many patients write after the clinic has closed, or from another time zone before travelling to Dubai. This assistant answers the routine questions at any hour (opening hours, location, treatments offered, how to request an appointment) and collects the appointment request for your team to confirm.

  • Collects name, preferred time and treatment; the team confirms every appointment.
  • Clinical questions, quotes and exceptions go to the team.
  • Never diagnoses and never gives prices.

Demo with sample conversations for a fictional clinic; hours and location are placeholders. In production it runs on your WhatsApp number and website with your own information, in English, Arabic and more, and your staff can take over any chat.
