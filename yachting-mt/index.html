How it would work for Yachting.mt

01

Owner writes, any time

On your WhatsApp (+356 9999 7714) or the website. The assistant replies in seconds, also evenings and weekends, in the owner's language.

02

It asks what you would ask

Name and contact, boat type and length, marina, the job (engines, antifouling & haul-out, teak, electrics, gelcoat & GRP, management, RHIB & tender storage, care plans), preferred dates and photos. For haul-outs: beam, draught, weight and time ashore, which the yard will ask for.

03

Hand-off to Stephen

A tidy summary lands on the request board and on your phone. You reply, arrange the visit and send the written quote. The assistant never quotes a price on its own.

04

Haul-out slot lined up

The owner picks preferred weeks; once you confirm the yard slot, the assistant tells the owner and sends reminders (paint and parts, lifting points, tanks low, someone to deliver the boat).

05

Owner kept in the loop

Status updates at each step until relaunch, so owners abroad don't need to chase you, and you don't need to chase them.

06

Safety first

Anything urgent (taking water, emergency) gets your phone number straight away and an instant alert to you, never a form.

Built on your existing channels: WhatsApp Business, your website quote form and email. It can also feed your owner portal. Set-up and rules (what it may say, when to alert you) are agreed with you first.
