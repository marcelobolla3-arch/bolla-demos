Scripted example Zügelteam Bergwind, a fictional moving company

Quote requests complete before you call back

This scripted chat shows what people ask when they move: are you free on the date, what you need for a quote, a viewing, packing materials, a furniture lift, the final clean, disposal and storage. It collects addresses, floor, lift, rooms and photos in the story. The quote and the price stay with the company. This page does not price a move.

Simulation: every reply is written in advance. Nothing is sent, nothing is booked, and this page is not connected to WhatsApp, a booking system, or a voice agent. If the address contains ?p=, that is only a visit label for BOLLA STUDIO. It does not change the page and it is not personalization.

This screen is a scripted chat example. It does not play audio. A voice assistant is not connected and is not running here. Voice, if commissioned separately, would be a different setup, not this page.

How to try this example

  1. Read the sample conversation. It is already on the screen.
  2. Tap a large question button, or type your own question in English and tap Send.
  3. Choose a move type, a date and the home size, then tap “Ask for a quote”. You will only see an example note. Nothing is sent.
